Digital Continuity

Deploy: Deadman Switch

If you go silent, your words don't. Deploy checks in with you on a schedule — and if you stop responding, it delivers your prepared messages automatically.

  • Configurable check-in intervals and inactivity timers
  • Delivers an encrypted Legacy payload with QR code
  • Self-hosted on your laptop, VPS, or Start9 server
  • Encrypted storage with single-use check-in links
